# Pehra brand kit

One brand across the website, the video, the README and the bot: paper, ink, a single saffron accent, Inter.

![Pehra banner](brand/banner.png)

## Name
**Pehra** (पहरा, "guard"). Always "Pehra", never "Invisible Heat" (that is the phenomenon: *invisible heat days*).

## Logo
| File | Use |
|---|---|
| [`brand/logo.svg`](brand/logo.svg) | Horizontal lockup: saffron dot, wordmark, पहरा |
| [`brand/mark.svg`](brand/mark.svg) | The dot alone, on paper, for icons and avatars |
| [`brand/banner.png`](brand/banner.png) | 1600×640 banner (README, social) |

The dot is the sun in the problem, the risk light on the Site Box, and the guard's lamp. Keep the ink ring on it.

Risk levels inside the product keep the heatcore colours (kam → khatarnak): `#2FB344 #FFC20E #FF8A1F #E8282B #A266B0`.

## Type
- **Inter** 400 to 800 for Latin. Headlines 700 to 800 with tight tracking (-0.03 to -0.05 em).
- **Noto Sans Devanagari** 600 to 700 for पहरा and Hindi.
- Numbers tabular. No serif, no decorative display faces.

## Motion
Springs with a 2 to 3% overshoot, entrance only, nothing loops. No glows, particles, gradients or crossfades. The saffron dot connects scenes.

## Illustration
Flat editorial illustrations generated with Codex in this palette (`web/img/ill-*.webp`, `video/public/gen/`), and the character sheets in `Pehra_Character_Sheets/` cut into poses (`web/img/char-*.webp`). Characters are illustrated people, never real ones; label them as such.

## Voice
Plain words, short sentences, Hindi in Roman script in the product. Say what it does and where the number came from. Never "saves lives", never "safe".
