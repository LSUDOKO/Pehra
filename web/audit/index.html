Invisible heat days

Days when heat and humidity together crossed the NIOSH work limit, but India's temperature-based heatwave rule would not have raised an alarm. 2021–2025, ten cities.

3,459

city-days with WBGT in the shade above the NIOSH limit for moderate work (28.2 °C) and no heatwave declared — 96% of all such days. 1,679 of them fell outside March–June, when heatwave bulletins do not run.

These numbers are a minimum.
  • A minimum: ERA5 (~25 km) smooths humid extremes.
  • IMD rule recreated on one grid cell per city; not IMD's official record.
  • Shade WBGT; sun counts are higher and listed separately.

How it was counted

Invisible heat day: WBGT in shade, 06-19 IST, above the NIOSH REL for moderate work on at least one hour, with no heatwave under the recreated IMD rule. In the sun, 11,858 city-days crossed the same limit.
